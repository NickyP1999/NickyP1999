Drop your alert sound files here.

The overlay looks for these filenames (referenced in js/config.js):
  follow.mp3
  sub.mp3
  cheer.mp3
  raid.mp3
  donation.mp3

You can use any .mp3 / .ogg / .wav. Rename your files to match, or change
the "sound" field for each alert in js/config.js. Leave a sound as "" for
a silent alert.

Free sound sources: https://freesound.org , https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/
